# LightEditor privacy policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

LightEditor ("the app", published on the App Store as LightEditor RAW) is a photo editor for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **LightEditor doesn't collect any data. Your photos and edits stay on your devices.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. LightEditor has no accounts, servers, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does. The developer receives no information about you, your photos or how you use the app.

## Your photos

LightEditor reads and edits photos in your Apple Photos library, with your permission. All decoding, editing and AI features (masks and object removal) run on your device. Edits are saved back to Photos non-destructively, so you can revert to the original at any time.

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs your photos and edits between your devices. That sync is Apple's service, covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by LightEditor.

When you export or share a photo, it goes only where you send it, through the system share sheet or a file location you choose.

## Data stored on your device

To stay fast, the app keeps some data in its own storage on your device: decoded image caches, AI masks, unsaved edit drafts, your presets and LUTs, and remote-editing pairing keys (in the Keychain). It never leaves the device, except as described below for remote editing. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Remote editing between your devices

If you turn on remote editing, one of your devices (for example your iPhone) can share its library with another nearby device (for example your iPad). The two connect directly over Wi-Fi, without going through the internet or any server. The connection is end-to-end encrypted, and a device can only connect after you approve it. Photos and edits sent over this link go only to your other device.

This is why the app asks for **Local Network** access. If you never turn on remote editing, the app makes no network connections.

## Permissions

- **Photos:** to show, edit and save your photos. You can choose limited access or change it at any time in Settings.
- **Local Network:** only for remote editing between your own devices.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
